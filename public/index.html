Freelance Escrow

Pay a freelancer in tUSD8 on the QDay Aevum testnet. The client locks the payment in escrow, the freelancer delivers, the client approves, and the payment releases. If the deadline passes with no delivery, the client gets the funds back.

Escrow contract

Paste the deployed FreelanceEscrow address. It is filled in from the project config when the deploy step has been done.

New invoice

Creating an invoice sends two transactions: first an approval for the escrow contract to move your tUSD8, then the invoice itself.
